Canasta 2-Deck Scoring

Coming Soon

Score Two Deck Canasta the easy way. Track melds, card counts, and team standings round by round — with full rules customization and game history.

Built for serious Canasta nights

Stop juggling pencils and scrap paper. This app is a purpose-built scorekeeper for the classic 4-player, 2-team Two Deck Canasta game, taking you from setup to final score with no math required.

Features

  • 4-player, 2-team gameplay with custom team names and colors
  • Guided round entry: melds → card counts → summary
  • Full canasta tracking: natural, mixed, wild, sevens, aces — plus red and black threes
  • Splash/wipeout scoring with optional perfect-cut bonuses
  • Six card-count layouts so data entry fits the way you play
  • Edit past rounds — see exactly how each correction changes the score
  • Player roster with persistent win/loss records and high-score tracking
  • Fully customizable scoring rules — save your house rules and load them anytime
  • Round-by-round dealer rotation and history
  • Resumes your game right where you left off

Why you'll like it

Designed by Canasta players for Canasta players. Whether you stick to traditional rules or play with your family's quirky variations, the customizable scoring engine handles it. The first scorekeeper that actually keeps up with the speed of your table.

Race to 8,500. Let the app do the math.
