Built for real games

Stop wrestling with paper scorepads and clunky calculators. Canasta Scorekeeper is the cleanest, fastest way to track a 4-player, 5-deck Canasta game from the first deal to the final hand.

Designed by players who actually play, every screen is tuned for the rhythm of a real game — quick taps between hands, no fiddly menus, and a layout that stays readable across the table.

Score a hand in seconds

  • Tap-to-cycle clean and mixed canastas for every rank
  • Multiple canastas per rank fully supported
  • Dedicated Seven and Wild canasta tracking
  • Red 3 bonus with threshold scaling, Black 3 penalties, and going-out bonus all handled automatically
  • Manual override or adjustment when a hand needs a custom score

Card counts that just work

  • Per-rank card entry with smart maximums based on melds and deck count
  • Side-by-side or stacked layouts to match how you like to play
  • Automatic sync between melds and card counts so you never double-enter

Track the whole game

  • Persistent round history with full per-team breakdowns
  • Tap any past round to see exactly where the points came from
  • Edit prior round scores with a clear delta showing what changed
  • Dealer rotation tracked automatically
  • Perfect Cut +100 bonus, one tap per player

Built for your table

  • 2 teams, 4 players, custom team names and colors
  • Roster of regular players with win/loss records over time
  • Configurable winning score (default 8500)
  • Game state persists between sessions — close the app mid-round and pick up exactly where you left off
  • Screen stays awake during play

Whether you're at the kitchen table or on vacation in Hawaii, this is the scorepad your Canasta night has been waiting for.
