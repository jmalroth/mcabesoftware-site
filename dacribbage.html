Score cribbage like a pro. Tap-fast hand entry, live pegging hints, and a real cribbage board that tracks every point so you can keep your eyes on the cards.

Keep score. Not arguments.

DaCribbage is the cleanest way to score a cribbage match on your phone. Whether you're playing 1-on-1 or partners, it tracks every hand, crib, and peg with a live on-screen board so you always know exactly where you stand.

Features

  • Live cribbage peg board — front and back pegs animate as scores go in
  • Fast hand entry for both teams plus the crib
  • Pegging hints that commit automatically after a short pause, so a quick burst of points stays in sync
  • Dealer and cutter tracking, hand by hand
  • Full hand-by-hand history you can expand and review
  • Resume mid-game right where you left off
  • Custom team names and color tags
  • Records view to look back at past games
  • Clean, distraction-free UI built natively in SwiftUI
  • No accounts. No ads. No internet required. Just score the game and play.

Perfect for casual players, weekly cribbage nights, family game tournaments, and anyone tired of fumbling with a physical peg board (or arguing about whose turn it is to deal).
