The smart way to keep score. Stop scribbling on napkins and arguing over bag penalties.

Built for the way Spades is actually played

Set up a game in seconds, enter bids and tricks, and let the app handle the rest — contract points, overtrick bags, the −100 penalty, nil bonuses, and blind nil. It just works.

Features

  • Full Spades scoring — bids, nil (100 pts), and blind nil (200 pts) handled automatically
  • Three bid input styles — numpad, stepper, or grid. Pick what feels right.
  • Customizable rules — adjust win/lose thresholds, bag penalties, and house-rule variants
  • Complete round history — tap any round to see exactly how the points broke down
  • Player & team management — drag-and-drop seating, custom team names and colors
  • Records & stats — win/loss tracking, high scores, and per-player leaderboards
  • Auto-resume — pick up right where you left off
  • Real-time validation — never enter an invalid bid or trick count again

Whether you play classic 500-to-win, track bags as points, or run your own house rules, Spades Scoring keeps up. Four players, two teams, zero math.
