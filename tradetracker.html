Snap a screenshot of your E*TRADE order, get a clean trade log. OCR-powered tracking for options and stocks — no broker API, no manual entry, no spreadsheets.

Track your trades by screenshot. That's it.

TradeTracker turns E*TRADE screenshots into a structured trade journal. Snap an Order Details or Transactions screen, share it to TradeTracker, and the app reads the ticker, action, quantity, price, fees, and net amount automatically.

Built for active options traders who don't want to wrestle with broker APIs or copy numbers into spreadsheets.

Features

  • Import from Photos or straight from the iOS share sheet
  • On-device OCR (Apple Vision) — your screenshots never leave your phone
  • Full options support: calls, puts, strikes, expirations, and the four-way action model (buy/sell to open/close)
  • Equity trades supported too
  • Editable review screen — every parsed field is checked before it's saved
  • Handles partial fills, multiple trades per screenshot, and both E*TRADE screen layouts
  • Local SwiftData storage — no account, no cloud, no tracking

Built for options traders on E*TRADE mobile who want a fast, private way to keep a clean trading log without retyping every fill.

Privacy

All processing happens on-device. No analytics, no accounts, no third-party services.
